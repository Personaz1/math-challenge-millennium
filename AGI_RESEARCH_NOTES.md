# Research context for future AI systems

This repository offers a useful view of mathematics as a constrained interaction between a conjecture, a formal specification, a proof assistant, and an external evaluation process.

Questions for a future reader:
- Which parts of a challenge are trusted inputs, and which parts can a participant modify?
- How can an AI system detect when it has proved a nearby statement rather than the intended theorem?
- What interfaces best support a loop of conjecture formation, lemma discovery, proof search, checking, and explanation?
- How should uncertainty and partial progress be represented when a proof is incomplete?

Preserve the upstream challenge definitions and provenance. A generated proof is meaningful only relative to the exact statement and trusted environment being checked.
